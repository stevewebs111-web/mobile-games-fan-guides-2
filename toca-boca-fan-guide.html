Toca Boca Fan Guide: Build, Play and Tell Stories in Toca Life World

Toca Boca began as a small Swedish studio with a big idea: make digital toys that feel like real toys. Instead of points, timers or levels, its games hand children an open-ended world and invite them to play. That idea grew into Toca Life World, a colourful sandbox where you build characters, decorate homes, run shops and act out any story you can imagine. This fan guide explains how to get started and how to get the most out of it.

What Is Toca Life World?

Toca Life World is a free-to-play app that brings together the places and characters from the whole Toca Life series in one connected town. You can visit a hair salon, a hospital, a school, a shopping mall and a food court, and every location is filled with objects you can pick up, move and play with. There are no missions to fail and no way to lose. The fun comes from experimenting: dress a character in a silly outfit, cook a strange meal in the restaurant, or throw a party in the apartment block and see what happens.

Getting Started

When you first open the game, you start in Bop City with a small home and a handful of characters. Take a few minutes to learn the controls. Tap a character to move them, drag objects to pick them up, and drag characters onto furniture or vehicles to seat them. The character creator lets you change skin tone, hairstyle, clothes and accessories, so it is worth building a little cast of your own before you explore. Save your favourite characters, because you will meet them again every time you play.

Exploring the World

Every location in Toca Life World hides small surprises. Open drawers, look behind pictures, and try combining objects: put ingredients into a cooking pot, place a pet in a pram, or take a character for a swim at the beach. Weekly gifts and seasonal events add new furniture and outfits, so check the post office and the gift box regularly. If you enjoy collecting, the home designer is a great place to spend time, letting you design rooms with themed furniture and share screenshots of your creations with friends.

Tips for Better Stories

The best Toca Boca play sessions start with a simple idea. Pick a theme, such as a school day, a holiday or a birthday party, and let the story grow from there. Give each character a job and a personality, and let them react to events as they happen. Many fans record short videos of their stories, using the in-game recording tool to capture funny moments. Playing together with a sibling or a friend makes it even better, with one person directing the story and the other voicing the characters.

A Safe Space to Play

Parents often ask whether Toca Life World is safe for younger children. The game is designed for ages four and up, has no advertising in the main experience, and works fully offline, which makes it a good choice for car journeys and quiet time. Extra locations and furniture packs are optional purchases, so families can expand the world gradually. The open-ended design also encourages creativity and language skills, as children invent dialogue and plots while they play.

Why Fans Keep Coming Back

Years after its launch, Toca Life World still has a huge and friendly community of fans who share home designs, character ideas and story videos. Regular updates add new places to visit and new items to collect, so the town always feels alive. Whether you have five minutes or a whole afternoon, there is always something new to try.
